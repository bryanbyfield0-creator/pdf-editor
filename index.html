Weeshee PDF Editor

Private · your files stay on this device

How to use Weeshee PDF Editor: Open a PDF, then pick a tool. Click to add text, draw a signature, highlight, or cover something with white-out. Drag items to move them, or pull a handle to resize. Use the page strip to rotate, reorder, remove, or change the paper size (Letter, A4, or a custom scale). Check “Smaller file” to compress images, then tap Download. Nothing is uploaded.
